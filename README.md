## What is Life Signs?

**Life Signs** is a proof of concept exploring what happens when game worlds are allowed to remember.

Games already create enormous amounts of history. Characters survive battles, travel with the player, change jobs, lose friends, move between places and experience events that may never happen in quite the same way again. Yet much of that history has little lasting meaning to the people supposedly living inside the world.

Life Signs asks a simple question:

> **What if the characters remembered?**

The idea is to take events that genuinely happen during play and allow them to become part of a character's continuing history. A person you rescued might remember being rescued. Someone who spent years travelling with you might recognise that shared past. Repeated encounters could gradually become familiarity, trust or resentment instead of every conversation beginning from nothing.

The aim is not simply to generate more dialogue. It is to make existing game worlds feel as though they have a past.

The game still creates the world, the events and the stories. Life Signs gives the people living there a way to remember them and respond naturally to what actually happened.

> **Let the game create events.  
> Let Life Signs remember them.  
> Let the characters talk about the lives they actually lived.**

## First Proof of Concept

### X4LifeSigns

The first Life Signs proof of concept is being developed for **X4: Foundations**.

X4LifeSigns is where the idea is being tested in an actual simulated game world, connecting persistent events and characters with memory, contextual interaction and natural conversation.

## Project Structure

This repository is the home of the wider **Life Signs** concept.

It exists to explain the idea, record the overall direction of the project and provide a central place connecting work carried out under the Life Signs name. The individual implementation repositories contain their own technical architecture, development documentation and game specific details.

## Project Status

Life Signs is currently an **early proof of concept**.

The first goal is simply to discover whether giving characters meaningful persistent memory can change how a simulated world feels to the person playing it.

If the player eventually stops thinking about databases, language models and software architecture and instead thinks:

> **"That person remembers me."**

then the experiment has done what it set out to do.
