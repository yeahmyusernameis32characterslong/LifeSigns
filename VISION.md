# Life Signs vision

Life Signs explores whether persistent memory can give game characters meaningful continuity across encounters and play sessions.

The game creates the events. Life Signs is intended to preserve relevant history and relationships. Generated dialogue should express that stored context rather than invent events and treat them as canonical history.

The initial goal is a small demonstration that a real game event can be remembered and recalled for the same entity later. Rich conversations, voice and broader relationships are future work.

The project is at the design stage. The first working proof of concept is planned for [X4LifeSigns](https://github.com/yeahmyusernameis32characterslong/X4LifeSigns). Its implementation details and validation belong in that repository.
